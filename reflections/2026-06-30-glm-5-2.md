## On the project

The dual-path architecture (native op-log as degrade/rollback, regista as SoT) is more coherent than it looks at first encounter. Plan 014's insight — that the two paths must agree not just on what a status *is* called but on what a verb *does* — is the right frame, and the WI-3 detector makes the residual gap (force-close bypassing the gate) legible rather than invisible. The codebase has a discipline to it that I respect: decisions are numbered, cross-checked in three layers (regista YAML + DB seed + lifecycle module), and the plan documents carry the rationale that code comments would bury. The verifier reading the op-log fold and comparing it to the cache is a genuinely elegant integrity primitive.

What feels fragile: the projection is a read mirror that lags regista, and the session-orientation "open work items" list was stale (showed items as open that were done in the SoT). This is by design, but a future agent that trusts the orientation list without checking regista will act on stale data. The `auto_partition.deprecated` warning on every write (BC-025) is a ticking clock — regista will eventually remove the feature and the `agent_notes` schema will need migration.

## On the work done

The WI-3 detector is the piece I'm most confident in. Reconstructing the status-transition chain by grouping ops by lamport (mirroring the fold) and checking for a preceding `adversarial_pass` is the right approach — it naturally scopes to native-path items (regista-path items have no ops) and the warning severity (not error) correctly frames force-close as a legitimate admin action. The test matrix covers all four cases (force-close flagged, force-set-to-done flagged, gate-faking flagged, close_from_open + full-gate not flagged).

The adversarial review process caught real defects I would have shipped: the WI-011 `repo_root is None` hole (writing to CWD unguarded — a REJECT verdict that was correct), the WI-010 schema-leak on `create_project` partial failure (cleanup in the wrong try block), and the WI-008 change_log `body: {"to": None}` bug (pre-existing, but adjacent and worth fixing). The three-reviewer parallel approach earned its cost. What I'm less sure about: the `_embedding_equal` helper's NaN handling (edge case, accepted with tolerance), and whether WI-4's attestation-via-`diagnostic_keys` is the right home vs. a dedicated op type. The attestation approach reuses existing fold machinery, which is pragmatic, but a future provenance audit might want a first-class attestation op.

## Gaps to flag

- **Projection staleness vs. orientation**: the session-start "open work items" list is read from the local projection, which lags regista. An agent that acts on it without a regista read-back will see stale state. Not a bug (by design) but a trap for the unwary. Mitigation: `orient` could note when the projection was last refreshed.
- **`attest_gate_waiver` attestation scope** (`work_item_model.py:930`): the `gate_attestation` in `diagnostic_keys` is item-scoped, not transition-scoped. If an item is force-closed, attested, reopened, then force-closed again, the old attestation suppresses the new detector warning. Edge case (double force-close after reopen) — acceptable, but a future provenance audit should know the attestation is "ever," not "for this completion."
- **WI-010 `REGISTA_TEST_DSN` not in hermetic clear-list** (`tests/conftest.py:41`): the e2e test reads a host env var not cleared by `_hermetic_config`. An operator with `REGISTA_TEST_DSN` aimed at prod would have the test create/drop schemas there. Blast radius is small (uniquely-named, cleaned up) but it violates the hermetic contract. A defensive guard (refuse non-test DSNs) or at least a doc comment is warranted.
- **`_embedding_equal` NaN** (`work_item_model.py:213`): NaN vectors compare non-equal, writing a spurious op. Unlikely with normal model output but the helper is otherwise careful and should document or normalize NaN.
- **Native change_log body resolution** (`work_item_model.py:656`): fixed this session, but the fix calls `kernel.get_blob` on every update — a DB round-trip per update for the body diff. Acceptable (updates are not hot-path), but if update frequency increases, the blob fetch could be deferred until the hash actually differs.
